# Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email **[ucnllc@gmail.com](mailto:ucnllc@gmail.com)**. We usually reply within two business days. In the app, open Settings and tap Contact Support; the email includes your app version so we can help faster.

## Common questions

**I bought Pro but the app does not show it.**
Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Purchases from the earlier Dr. Scan app carry over.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Keepscan. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

**I deleted a document by mistake.**
Open Settings and tap Recently Deleted. Deleted documents stay there for 30 days.

**Where are my documents stored?**
On your device. If you turn on iCloud Sync (Pro), they are also stored in your own private iCloud account. We cannot see them. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).
